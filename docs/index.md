## Why Spiritbound exists

A priest keeps a few long buffs up all the time: Power Word: Fortitude, Inner Fire, Divine Spirit if you have the talent, and Shadow Protection for some fights. Each lasts long enough to forget. Prayer of Fortitude needs candles and Levitate needs feathers, and both run out at the wrong moment.

Spiritbound watches all of that. It shows a card when a buff is missing or about to run out, and you click the card to cast it. It warns you before your candles or feathers run out. One key buffs your group. It only loads on priests.

## What it does

- **Buff cards.** A card when Fortitude, Inner Fire, Divine Spirit or Shadow Protection is missing or running out. Click it to cast. A Prayer from another priest counts. [More](features/buffs.html)
- **Buff key.** Out of combat, each press casts what a card asks for, then Fortitude, Divine Spirit and Shadow Protection on the next group member who is missing it. [More](features/buff-key.html)
- **Reagents.** A card when Holy or Sacred Candles or Light Feathers run low. [More](features/reagents.html)
- **Travel panel.** Press Escape: Levitate and your Hearthstone sit beside the game menu. [More](features/travel.html)
- **Well-Rested and Well Fed.** A warning and a countdown before they run out. [More](features/well-rested-and-well-fed.html)
- **Explanations.** Hover over a card or button to read what it does and why. [More](features/explanations.html)
- **Your layout.** Every frame can be moved, snapped to a grid and resized. [More](features/frames-and-minimap.html)
- **Three skins.** Spirit, Blizzard and Flat, with an emblem that follows your spec. [More](features/skins.html)

## The Bound family

Spiritbound is one of the Bound addons, one per class, built on the same core: Lightbound (paladin), Chronobound (mage), Stormbound (shaman), Packbound (hunter), Spiritbound (priest), Felbound (warlock) and Dreambound (druid). They look like their class but work the same way, so if you know one, you know them all.
