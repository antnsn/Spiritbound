# Skins

Spiritbound comes in three looks. They all work the same way, only the frames look different, so you can match the rest of your UI.

## The three skins

| Skin | Looks like | Good with |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Spirit** (default) | Twilight-violet panels with a silver edge, a soft mist, feather marks in the corners and a faint priest crest behind each card. A card that needs you has a slow pale glow at its edge. | The default UI, or if you like the priest look. |
| **Blizzard** | The game's own grey and silver tooltips, dialogs and buttons. No crest on the cards. | A mostly default UI. |
| **Flat** | Thin black edges on dark grey, Arial Narrow text, no crest. | EllesmereUI, ElvUI and other flat UI packs. |

The screenshots on this site show the Blizzard skin.

## How to switch

![The Skin page in the options]({{ '/screenshots/options-skin.png' | relative_url }})

1. Type `/spirit` and open the **Skin** page.
2. Click the **Skin** button to step through the skins (right-click goes back).
3. Click **Reload now**, or type `/reload` later. The new skin shows after the reload.

The skin is saved with your other Spiritbound settings.

## The emblem follows your spec

Spiritbound's emblem is a hooded priest. It shows the white hood for Holy and Discipline, and the purple hood once you know a Shadow talent spell: Shadowform, Vampiric Embrace, Mind Flay or Silence. It changes by itself when you respec.

The emblem is used for the logo in the options window, the minimap button and the crest behind the cards.

## What changes, and what doesn't

A skin changes the frames: colors, borders, fonts, buttons and check boxes in the options window, the cards and the travel panel.

Red, for a buff that is missing or a reagent at 0, means the same in every skin.

## Things to know

**Why does it need a reload?**
Spiritbound builds its frames once, when you log in. Switching in the middle of a session would leave some frames in the old look and some in the new one, so the switch waits for the next reload.
