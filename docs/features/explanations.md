# Explanations

Hover over a Spiritbound card or a travel panel row and the tooltip says, in a sentence or two, what it is for. Turn it off once you know it all.

## What it does

The explanations show in the tooltips of:

- the four [buff cards](buffs.html), also when a card offers a cast (the tooltip then names the spell and ends with **Click to cast (out of combat)**),
- the [reagents card](reagents.html),
- the [Well-Rested and Well Fed](well-rested-and-well-fed.html) cards,
- the Levitate and Hearthstone rows of the [travel panel](travel.html).

The texts:

| Where | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Power Word: Fortitude card | Power Word: Fortitude gives more Stamina, so more health. Keep it on yourself, and on your friends too. |
| Inner Fire card | Inner Fire gives you armor and attack power for a while. Keep it up, especially when you're hit. |
| Divine Spirit card | Divine Spirit gives more Spirit, so your mana comes back faster between fights. |
| Shadow Protection card | Shadow Protection reduces shadow damage. Worth it in fights against shadow casters. |
| Reagents card | Prayer of Fortitude buffs your whole group at once and uses a candle (Holy Candle, later Sacred Candle), sold by reagent vendors. Levitate lets you float and fall slowly. It uses a Light Feather, sold by reagent vendors. |
| Well-Rested card | Sleeping in a Cozy Sleeping Bag gives Well-Rested: more experience for a while. |
| Well Fed card | Eating buff food gives Well Fed: better stats, and on Forever more experience too. |
| Levitate (travel panel) | Levitate lets you (or a friend) walk on water and fall slowly for 2 minutes. Uses a Light Feather. |
| Hearthstone (travel panel) | Your Hearthstone takes you back to the inn you set it to. Talk to an innkeeper to change it. |

## How to use it

Hover over a card or a row. That's all.

## Settings

In `/spirit`, page **Skin**, section **General**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain things in tooltips | On | Shows the explanations. Off: no explanations; a card that offers a cast still shows the spell name and "Click to cast". |

`/spirit explain` turns it on or off from chat.

![The Skin page, with the General switches]({{ '/screenshots/skins/blizzard/options-skin.png' | relative_url }})

## Good to know

**No tooltip while frames are unlocked.**
While you move frames, the cards don't show their explanations, so they don't get in the way.
