# Travel panel

Press Escape and a small **Travel** panel opens beside the game menu, with Levitate and your Hearthstone. Click one to cast it.

## What it does

- The panel shows whenever the game menu opens, out of combat.
- **Levitate** is listed under **PRIEST**. Before you learn it, the row is greyed out and says **level 34**; its tooltip says where to learn it (a priest trainer).
- **Hearthstone** is listed below, with the inn it is set to, for example `Hearthstone: Gallows' End Tavern`. The row only shows while you carry a Hearthstone.
- Click a row to cast it. The game menu closes after the click.

![The travel panel beside the game menu]({{ '/screenshots/travel.png' | relative_url }})

The shot shows the panel in the Blizzard skin, on a priest who hasn't learned Levitate yet.

## How to use it

1. Press Escape.
2. Click **Levitate** or **Hearthstone** in the panel.

## Settings

In `/spirit`, page **Travel**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show the travel panel when the game menu opens | On | Turns the panel on or off. |

![The Travel page in the options]({{ '/screenshots/options-travel.png' | relative_url }})

## Good to know

**Not in combat.**
The panel only opens out of combat, and it hides when a fight starts. Spells cast from it need you out of combat anyway.

**Can I move the panel?**
No. It always sits to the right of the game menu.

**Levitate needs a Light Feather.**
The panel doesn't check your feathers. The [reagents card](reagents.html) warns you when they run low.
